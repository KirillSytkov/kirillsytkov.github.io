Privacy Policy

Pintory: Beer Diary · Effective October 27, 2026

Pintory is a beer diary app. The developer is Kiryl Sytskou ("we"). Contact: kirill.sytkov.dev@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your entries and photos stay on your iPhone. The app does not connect to any server of ours, and we never see your data.
  • There is no account or sign-up. No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • We do not collect any data from the app.

What stays on your device

The records you enter (beer name, brewery, style, rating, taste, tags, notes, dates), the photos you add to records and to the bar wall, and the app settings. All of it is stored in the app on your iPhone.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo; access is requested at that moment and you can revoke it in Settings. Photos from your library are added through the system photo picker, so the app does not get access to your whole library. Photos are stored locally in the app.

Export and import

You can export your data (CSV or an archive with photos) and import an archive. The file is created, saved or shared by you; the developer never receives it.

Purchases

Premium (subscription or one-time purchase) and promo codes are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record in the app. Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.

Children

The app is about alcohol and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
